BRAVO TEAM

Alpha finds them. Bravo finishes them.
How a shift runs. Tap a specialist, then a glowing tile to move (2 AP each, move = 1 AP). Tiles only move you — everything you can operate shows up as a button in the action bar the moment you stand beside it: doors, the furnace F (heats the site), the breaker B (site power), searches, the salt line, ? anchor candidates, and the downed Alpha (SHOULDER THE ALPHA — no tagging, you are the extraction). Your crew only sees where they are facing: the headlamp beam in the dark, ordinary sight where a room light is on. Confirm the entity from its Tells in the Journal, place the rite reagents at the Anchor A, meet the rite's condition, and Channel until the banishment banks. In-game, the ? button opens the full field guide.

WHAT YOU CAN SEE
The site is dark. You see what your crew's headlamps reach — a cone in the direction each specialist last moved, blocked by walls — and any room whose light is on. Switching a room light on reveals it wholly, but costs Dread and tells the entity where you are. Rooms you have already been through stay dimly remembered; anything moving in them does not.

CONTROLS
Tap a card or token to select · tap a glowing tile to move · everything you can use is a named button in the action bar · drag to pan · pinch or wheel to zoom · SND toggles audio.
Keys: 1/2/3 select a specialist · TAB cycles · ENTER ends the turn · H hide · F sprint · ESC closes panels.

THE NIGHT
Confirm the entity from its Tells (JNL) — the report can be wrong. Place its rite reagents at A, meet the rite's condition, Channel until it banks. Dread only rises; from 60 the dial shows Hunt odds. During a Hunt: hide, break line of sight, or finish the rite — completion ends the Hunt instantly. Then shoulder the Alpha and walk everyone to the van.
